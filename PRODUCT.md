# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Global "vibe-coders" and non-technical founders who shipped software with AI
(Claude, Cursor, Lovable, Bolt) and now need someone independent to tell them
whether it's sound — before they take money, users, or investment on it.
Secondary: small teams whose AI-built product is already live and creaking.

Context when they arrive: anxious, technically out of their depth, burned or
worried about being burned. They cannot evaluate code themselves; they are
evaluating *trustworthiness* from the page alone. The job to be done: "verify
what I built, fix what's broken, and stand behind it."

## Product Purpose

The Zha Foundry is the independent party that verifies AI-built software.
The funnel is a ladder: **Assay** (free audit) → **Cast** (build new) /
**Forge** (rescue) → **Care** (subscription upkeep) → **Zha Standard**
(published bar), pointing long-term at a certification/warranty business.

Positioning is always "independent verification," never "we fix AI's bad
code." Success looks like qualified Assay submissions from visitors who
arrived skeptical and left convinced by the craft of the page itself —
the site is the first proof of the standard.

## Brand Personality

**Monumental, precise, quiet.**

- Monumental: the page carries weight — foundry scale, real physical metaphor
  (the page IS a pour, cooling 1,084.6 °C → 24.0 °C from hero to footer).
- Precise: instrument-grade details — gauges, readouts, seals, checklists.
  Everything measured, nothing decorative for its own sake.
- Quiet: no urgency tricks, no hype adjectives, no exclamation marks. The
  voice of someone who inspects for a living and doesn't need to shout.

Truth constraints (non-negotiable): no prices on the site (fixed quote after
free assay), no fake proof or testimonials ever, copy stays honest to
solo-founder reality. The Stepped Z's derivation from the proto-Tamil ழ
ladder glyph is deliberately private — never explained in public copy.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS template**: cream hero, gradient blob, three-column pricing
  grid, testimonial carousel, "Trusted by 10,000+" walls.
- **Dev-tool dark neon**: Vercel/Linear-clone dark mode, electric gradients,
  terminal cosplay, bento grids of features.
- **Agency flash**: Awwwards scroll-jacking spectacle that buries the offer;
  motion as fireworks instead of narrative.
- **Corporate consulting**: stock photography, suits, vague enterprise
  language, trust-badge rows, "solutions" copy.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the first assay.** Every detail is evidence of the standard
   being sold; sloppy craft anywhere contradicts the entire pitch.
2. **Metaphor with physics, not decoration.** The foundry system (heat, pour,
   cooling, seals, stamps) must behave consistently — one heat ramp, one
   temperature narrative — or it reads as theater.
3. **Struck, not drawn.** Square caps, miter joins, stamped and engraved
   treatments. Nothing rounded-friendly, nothing hand-wavy.
4. **Quiet confidence over persuasion.** No manufactured urgency, no social
   proof theater. Claims are checkable; absences (prices, testimonials) are
   deliberate and explained.
5. **Motion tells the cooling story.** Animation exists to advance the
   pour narrative and clarify state — never as an entrance reflex — and
   every effect ships a designed static fallback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: **WCAG 2.2 AA.**

- Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast in both themes (foundry daylight + foundry night).
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` coverage: every effect has a designed static
  fallback (heat lines render lit, checkboxes render checked, doors render
  open) — not just disabled animation.
- Keyboard-navigable throughout; visible focus states on all interactive
  elements including the theme toggle and Assay form.
- Canvas/WebGL layers are decorative-only and must never carry sole meaning.
